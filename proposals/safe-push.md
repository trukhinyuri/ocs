# Safe Push for the canonical branch

**Status** Proposed. The draft owner is responsible for the admission policy. Domain owners and an independent assurance reviewer are responsible for the claims they sign. The policy applies to contributors, automated candidate generators and changes to the policy itself.

## Problem and evidence

At commit `3facf571910e0799539a77a1a5112e6609a9a5ca` the reference suite qualified 394 obligations across 245 atomic promises, including 14 observations on one Kubernetes target. It exercised 66 positive and negative controls. The repository has 213 tracked paths at that revision. The [qualification guide](../assurance/README.md) distinguishes model proofs, program refinements, artifact observations and deployment measurements. The [workload policy](../stand/kubernetes/policy.json) accepts a p95 of up to 2,000 ms. The qualifying run measured 24.500401 ms across 32 reads with concurrency four. A later 40 ms result would meet the existing ceiling and violate a strict comparison with that observation.

The [Makefile](../Makefile) supplies default proof and protocol revisions. The [launcher](../assurance/launch.py) can use a verifier independently chosen by the operator. The inspected checkout has no configured remote and no tracked forge configuration. Running a qualification command locally does not establish which revision a canonical receiver will publish.

The retained local report took about 180 s and the full report about 218 s on the qualified arm64 machine. Wall time is the difference between the signed or recorded start and finish fields of each individual run. Inspect `out/qualification/full/latest/evidence/stand.json` at `stand.load` for the raw read samples and `out/qualification/{local,full}/latest/report.json` for the populations and timestamps. Use these as starting observations when measuring verifier cost. The admission policy itself needs a separately enrolled performance baseline.

The affected rules are [T-01 through T-04](../spec/conformance.md) and [L-04 and L-06](../spec/profiles/platforms.md). All 31 [existing scenarios](../conformance/scenarios.json) remain in scope, including `edition-compatibility`, `extension-evolution`, `operational-evidence`, `capacity-and-recovery` and the published `recover-after-input-error` case of `status-call`. The proposed admission decision belongs to release engineering. The existing wire contract and application invocation remain compatible.

## Meaning of admission

A qualified claim names its stable ID, semantics, population, interface and role, environment assumptions, acceptable and forbidden outcomes, progress or cost obligation, required evidence, falsifier and owner. Each criterion and required evidence level is protected by the previously admitted policy. Empirical claims also identify units, measurement method, target, uncertainty, validity period and comparators. Qualitative claims require an explicit rubric, examples, exceptions and accountable domain review. Keep the existing [atomic catalogue](../assurance/obligations.json), [scope](../assurance/scope.json) and [result format](../assurance/result.schema.json) and link admission evidence to their identifiers.

The established trust root identifies the previously admitted commit, the meanings of its claims and the release authority. An admission receipt binds that parent, the exact proposed commit and complete tree, the policy revision, versioned proof kernels and axioms, declared targets, artifacts, signed observations, their time and revocation state, and the result for every applicable obligation. The outcome has three values.

| Outcome | Required evidence | Ref update |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted | Every old guarantee is preserved, every new obligation is discharged at its prescribed level and every protected metric satisfies its comparison. All certificates and required independent decisions are current for this exact candidate. | Atomically publish the certified commit if the canonical parent still matches. |
| Rejected | A reproducible counterexample, incompatible guarantee, worsened protected observation, invalid certificate or violation of the policy. | Keep the parent and record the failed criterion and evidence. |
| Undetermined | Missing proof, unknown applicability, unjustified exemption, unavailable platform, expired observation, timeout, indeterminate comparison or unreviewed real-world premise. | Keep the parent. An explicit disposition identifies exactly what would resolve it. |

The invariant to prove about a receiver is that every canonical transition from an enrolled parent has a valid admission receipt and preserves every previously accepted obligation on its original domain. The base case verifies the enrolled checkpoint. For the induction step, the sole writer verifies the candidate with the old policy, checks the expected parent and publishes exactly that commit. Transitivity of the proved preservation relations covers earlier accepted editions. The initial root, sole-writer enforcement and verification of the real receiver binary are distinct premises. This is a proposed theorem and a proof outline. Establishing it for an implementation requires a machine-checked refinement through its actual ref-update and persistence mechanisms.

A universal total decider for arbitrary behavioral properties of arbitrary programs cannot exist. Admitting only candidates with checkable certificates can be sound under named premises and can leave a correct candidate undetermined. A finite experiment cannot establish a property over every physical execution. Absolute immunity to compromise of the trust root, scientific truth for unformulated requirements, universal performance non-regression and independent verification using only mutable candidate bytes remain unsatisfied as universal claims. The ledger must retain them as such. [Computability theorem](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-045j-automata-computability-and-complexity-spring-2011/7aead2c728dd3d5a737d832811ef97e6_MIT6_045JS11_lec09.pdf)

## Protect meanings and every repository input

| Subject | Obligation before admission |
|---|---|
| Requirements, schemas, standards text and governance | Preserve stable identities, scope, licensed uses, semantics, permissions, fairness assumptions, success, refusal, ordering, recovery and resource bounds. Link each normative paragraph to its formal interpretation or named independent validation. Check for contradictions and vacuity. Record disputed interpretations and observable counterexamples. |
| Source, generated code, binaries and state | Exercise public entry points and old/new consumers. Prove critical paths against the selected semantics, including failure, concurrency, persistent state and migration. Connect the theorem to the deployed bytes and ABI with a verified translation, checked translation witness or machine-code proof whenever a binary guarantee is required. Rebuild generated output and check its actual inputs. |
| Tests, data, models, benchmarks and reported results | Retain independent oracles, accepted results, unfavorable outcomes and contrary evidence. Bind methods, workloads, platform, raw observations, complete manifests, clocks, eligibility and freshness. Exercise restored improvements as positive controls and weakened guarantees as negative controls. |
| Proofs, checkers, policies, build recipes, weights or data, dependencies and forge rules | Record exact versions, provenance, applicability and trust boundaries. Apply the old policy to any proposed change of definitions, axioms, verifier, skip rules, weights, tolerances, permitted exemptions, test selection, comparator, dependency closure or signing procedure. |
| Documentation, examples, assets, release inventory and new types of file | Account for every tree entry, mode, link, external object, artifact and build input. Detect removals and type changes. Execute runnable examples. Trace substantive claims to a source and evidence. Review ambiguities, unnecessary complexity and unsupported assertions. A new kind of object needs a classifier and applicable obligations before promotion. |

For an old interface, old admissible inputs and environmental conditions remain admissible. Every new observable behavior on that domain must satisfy the old guarantees, after projection of new optional fields. Required successful operations and required refusals must remain reachable. Liveness is proved under the original delivery, fairness and recovery assumptions. An implementation that refuses all work can preserve a safety-only formula and still break the contract. Also preserve confidentiality, resource isolation and service behavior for a consumer that never enables the new feature. For stateful composition, prove the guarantees of each supplier meet the assumptions of the next component.

Equivalent wording or a stronger implementation can enter with a checked mapping to the earlier meaning. An independently versioned optional behavior can coexist with the original if its non-interference is established. A discovered unsafe or contradictory old rule needs an erratum, affected-use review and suspension of its affected qualification. Keep the earlier text, proofs and dissent in history. If honoring the correction would violate an indispensable old commitment, strict monotonic admission of that change remains blocked. A version label by itself supplies no evidence of preservation.

Treat authorship as irrelevant. Require an observable purpose, traceable claims, executable examples where applicable and independent review of meaning. Record the alternatives and why additional complexity is justified by a requirement. Counts of changed lines, tests, references, models voting for a patch, or prose-likelihood scores establish none of those properties.

## Scientific evidence, critical applications and tools

For each substantive new or changed claim, register its hypothesis, domain, falsification condition and method before seeing candidate results. Cite applicable primary evidence and contrary findings. Identify assumptions shared by ostensibly independent methods. Publish raw data, software, instruments and calibration, protocols, seeds, sampling, exclusions, uncertainty and every trial outcome. Obtain an independent reproduction with the same material and, for an empirical claim where replication is required, a replication with new observations. Publish unresolved disagreement. The necessary sample size, diversity of studies and independence follow the claim, hazard, effect size, uncertainty and tolerated decision error. A universal fixed number of papers or unsuccessful attempts to falsify a result cannot establish truth. [Research reproducibility and replicability](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/2)

For a spacecraft or life-support application, the adopting project supplies the intended use, system boundary, hazards, credible faults including common causes, severity, controls, environmental limits and integration with humans, sensors, actuators, hardware and other software. Trace each hazard control to an independently verified requirement and integration evidence. Exercise degraded modes, containment, recovery and the response when a sensor, timing assumption or operator action is wrong. Verify timing, fault tolerance and observability on the named target. Maintain configuration control, independent verification and validation, release authority, residual-risk decisions, field monitoring, withdrawal and incident correction. The project safety authority decides whether that evidence suffices for the specific use and applicable standards. [Space software assurance and independent validation](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-87398) · [Space software product assurance](https://ecss.nl/standard/ecss-q-st-80c-rev-2-software-product-assurance-30-april-2025/)

Maintain a versioned method and threat register. For each change, map both its direct and transitive claims to evidence methods, the distinct fault each method could detect, and a reviewed justification of applicability. Use complete exploration on specified finite domains, induction or compositional proofs for unbounded protocols, information-flow and authority proofs for data separation, and source or binary analysis for memory, arithmetic and concurrency. Include fault injection, differential and metamorphic cases, structural coverage where the applicable assurance authority requires it, and tests of the oracles themselves. Check obligations for both progress and safety. A newly recognized fault model or a retracted scientific result reopens affected claims across releases. Counting tools or papers cannot establish completeness for undiscovered phenomena.

Select an existing checker by the exact theorem it establishes, the exact binary or build to which the theorem applies, assumptions, license and independent replay. The [current tool inventory](../assurance/tooling.json) and [wire proof chain](../assurance/wire/README.md) are starting points. Candle has an end-to-end soundness theorem for exported higher-order logic facts from its machine code under the stated model. CompCert proves preservation for its supported C semantics and compiler passes with a documented boundary. Each can serve a specific obligation only where its theorem applies. A proof-search engine or LLM may propose a witness. Admission relies on an independently controlled checker for the actual proposition. The requirements must also cover the glue, parsers, data extraction, binary loading, clocks, platform and admission receiver. Untested or unverified trusted dependencies keep a claim requiring end-to-end proof open. [Verified HOL prover](https://cakeml.org/candle/) · [Verified compiler semantics](https://compcert.org/man/manual001.html)

## Performance and iterative selection

Register every protected metric with its direction, units, population, measurement point, reference, uncertainty and platform. Include end-to-end and tail latency, deadline misses, error and cancellation behavior, throughput, memory, CPU, I/O, energy and recovery where relevant. Apply the same discipline to verification time and resources. Preserve legacy workloads and refer to each historical qualified envelope or a proven transitive equivalent. Additional capabilities add comparison cells. Travel across incomparable hardware, firmware, scheduler, data or measurement conventions requires a separately qualified relation or a fresh comparison under the original conditions.

* For deterministic cost, prove the candidate cost is no greater than the reference cost on every protected input and machine state for each minimization metric. Reverse the direction for maximization metrics. Prove system-level bounds including blocking, interference and scheduling for a hard deadline. A tighter upper bound alone can coexist with a slower implementation on an input where the old implementation was fast. [Worst-case execution time](https://www.cs.fsu.edu/~whalley/papers/tecs07.pdf)
* For the requested strict observed rule, any valid comparable registered result that gets worse rejects that candidate. Retain the result even if a rerun performs better. Averages, tolerances or a weighted score cannot cancel it. Missing or incomparable observations leave the decision undetermined. A measured guarantee covers its recorded conditions. If a registrar requires a mathematical guarantee for future executions, support it separately or keep it open.
* Define metric comparison cells in advance, preserve raw measurements, pair base/candidate trials, control order and thermal state and evaluate repeated baseline against itself. Define valid measurements and exclusions before the run. For inferential claims use independent evaluation, account for repeated looks and multiple metrics and preserve the experimental error budget. An iterative optimizer sees development evidence. An independent evaluator controls withheld cases and releases limited feedback, with every attempt recorded. If the entire benchmark must be public, use fresh independent observations and seal the protocol before the candidate is built. Replaying a public workload supplies reproducibility and does not create an independent validation population. [Adaptive data analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26250683/)

## Authority, recovery and efficiency

Keep public policies, evidence formats, verification sources, signed trust metadata, dependency manifests, reproducible build recipes, test inputs and platform adapters in the repository. Arrange offline access to the pinned dependencies and evidence where licensing permits. Keep private signing material, the initial trusted public-key fingerprint, anti-rollback state, an independent durable audit copy and the canonical writer under independent control. A copy under the candidate author's exclusive write access cannot establish its own authenticity. Separate author, builder, verifier, evidence custodian and publishing authority. Select thresholds and independent custodians against the documented compromise model. Threshold signatures authenticate participants. The theorem, evidence and domain review establish what they approved. [Threshold root rotation and rollback checks](https://theupdateframework.github.io/specification/latest/) · [Isolated build provenance](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.2/build-requirements)

Run candidate code and untrusted proof search in disposable environments with read-only inputs, bounded resources and no release keys or write access to the verifier, audit log or canonical repository. A separately administered verifier reads evidence as data, checks its schema and cryptographic bindings, and issues a verdict for the exact candidate. Keep the build and experimental inputs reproducible per platform. Document how many independent key holders, controllers or machines must remain uncompromised for the admission invariant to hold.

Use a staged, single canonical commit if the forge can publish its exact independently admitted object. Otherwise verify every newly reachable commit before updating the ref, or use a canonical receiver that supports this operation. Bind parents, tree and repository identity. On a concurrent parent update, invalidate the pending admission and requalify the resulting integration commit. Signed receipts and an append-only history support idempotent retries and recovery between writing the receipt and publishing the ref. Consumers validate the enrolled root, policy epoch, exact commit and artifact, freshness and revocation independently when utmost assurance is required.

A hosted forge adapter must require the independently owned publishing check for protected branches, block direct or forced updates and deletion, remove bypass actors, require the selected independent reviews, invalidate stale approvals and evaluate the merge-group candidate. The publisher reports success only after it adjudicates the entire receipt. Missing, neutral, skipped and cancelled evidence never yield publisher success. Verify the active configuration by readback and test the actual ref transition with disposable positive and negative fixtures. Include administration, service credentials and changes to the rules in the trust boundary. A controlled Git receiver can use a pre-receive check under an independently administered account. [Hosted branch rules](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/available-rules-for-rulesets) · [Hosted check semantics](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/how-tos/merge-and-close-pull-requests/troubleshooting-required-status-checks) · [Git receiver](https://git-scm.com/docs/git-receive-pack)

A verifier change is a separate promotion. The old guardian checks a transition certificate preserving every enrolled meaning, trust premise and required evidence level. A second independent review replays historic certificates and positive and negative controls, requiring meaningful successes as well as refusals. Prove the replacement is sound for the enrolled language and that its implemented parser, checker and publication behavior match that proof, including equivalence for changed representations. Authorize root changes under both the old and new root policies and advance a monotonic epoch. If the old trusted base is compromised, suspend publication, revoke affected evidence and recover from an independently enrolled checkpoint. No claimed assurance survives a known invalid premise without reassessment.

An outstanding confirmed counterexample is an admission blocker for its entire affected scope. Require a durable link to the reproducer, affected claims, proposed strengthening, independent replay of the fix and preservation of the previous positives. Unknown defects cannot be discovered on demand by a contributor or an automated agent. The enforceable duty begins when contrary evidence is available.

Accelerate search by rejecting bad candidates early and running independent obligations in parallel. Reuse proof objects under complete dependency keys covering semantics, axioms, full transitive inputs, tool identities, build options, target, reference, trust epoch and expiry. Verify the reuse witness on the exact candidate. For every canonical commit, recheck the full ledger and perform each required per-commit observation. Unknown dependencies invalidate reuse. Benchmark the verification pipeline against full replay on the actual platforms. Define admission queue time, verifier cost and throughput under its workload, including simultaneous arrivals and failed workers. Iterative search can be inexpensive on contributor branches while canonical admission retains every required check. Protect the verifier's own performance cells by the same rules. If a newly required check and the retained time bound cannot both be met on the registered hardware, admission remains undetermined or rejected according to the evidence.

## Acceptance experiments and rollout

| Fixture | Required decision or observable behavior |
|---|---|
| Delete a previously required test and its index entry. Weaken its formula or change its expected outcome. | Reject with the original stable claim ID from the enrolled policy. |
| Introduce a contradictory requirement, a narrower domain, an unjustified additional axiom, or a service that refuses every formerly successful request. | Reject a witnessed violation or hold for a missing consistency, realizability or domain proof. |
| Pass a looser performance ceiling while worsening a protected observation. Select a later favorable repeat. | Reject, retaining the original unfavorable observation. |
| Change the trust root, gate, policy or observer in the candidate, or submit a receipt for another parent or binary. | Hold the ref at the enrolled parent. Identify the failed binding or missing transition proof. |
| Introduce an unclassified path, alter a transitive input, lose one required target, or use an expired or revoked certificate. | The relevant obligation remains open until newly discharged. |
| Submit two candidates from one parent or lose the publisher after receipt creation. | Publish only a valid transition from the actual parent. Consistent recovery must work without duplicated or unattributed promotions. |
| Discover a credible counterexample after acceptance. | Quarantine affected uses, preserve the witness, identify dependent releases and publish the disposition. Require a protected regression case, a proposed repair and complete requalification for restoring the claim. Any person or agent can report the finding. |
| Supply an equivalent implementation, a stronger proof or an isolated optional improvement. | Publish if the inherited guarantees, every metric and the new obligations are satisfied. Keep old state and old clients interoperable. |
| Replay the same accepted and rejected fixtures on each claimed OS, architecture and ABI. | Agreed decisions with the specified platform-specific evidence. Unavailable targets stay open. Explicitly test file modes and paths, clocks, dependency resolution and failure behavior on each target. |

Seed the ledger from the published protocol and assurance pins, verify the full tree inventory and classify uncovered claims. Run the proposed decision in observation mode on historical good and bad changes. Validate a disposable protected branch, independent root rotation, loss and restore, cross-platform replay and consumer verification. Activate the canonical writer only after the evidence covers these transitions and the owner enrolls its trust root. The draft owner and application safety authority retain their separate responsibilities. This proposal introduces a reviewable design. No certified receiver, mission qualification or externally enforced rule is asserted by this document.
