# Governance

This is a local working draft. Its name and number identify this document series. They do not imply accreditation, compatibility with any other format, or authorization to operate an external system.

The repository owner maintains the draft. Contributors propose changes in the open issue/patch format described in CONTRIBUTING.md. The maintainer records the decision, reasons, affected roles and compatibility impact. An implementer may challenge a rule with a reproducible counterexample. Record dissent and alternatives in the proposal before adopting a disputed change.

A correction to examples or prose can be reviewed directly. A new required behavior, permission or breaking change needs a proposal, a migration path and an impact review. A feature progresses from proposed, to experimental with a running example, to candidate with black-box cases, to stable after two independent implementations demonstrate the same rule and its failures. Mark the particular feature and binding. Maturity does not transfer to unrelated profiles.

Contributors retain their copyright and submit original or appropriately licensed work under the repository license. Public release, naming policy, changes to licensing and designation of maintainers are explicit owner decisions. Record sensitive issues through the private reporting procedure in SECURITY.md.
